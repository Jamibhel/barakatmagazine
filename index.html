A LITTLE SOMETHING FOR YOU ❤️

Dearest Aunty Barakat,

Growing up, you were more than just our big sister. You were basically our second mum. 😂❤️

Happy 34th birthday, Aunty Barakat. We love you plenty.

Abdullah, Jamiu & Nike ❤️

English Translation:
(Àbẹ̀fẹ́! The one whom we plead and strive to love, the one sought after by all with deep devotion. The graceful eyed one, child of radiant honor. Àbẹ̀fẹ́ Ọmọlábákẹ́, the precious child destined to be pampered, cherished, and treated like royalty. Your graceful steps radiate honor, and your smile brings boundless joy to the home. You will live long, you will prosper, you will eat the fruits of your labor in health, and your joy will never run dry! 🤍✨)
